A little about me

Media, culture, and meaningful stories.

I'm a lecturer in the Faculty of Digital Communication and Hotel & Tourism at BINUS University, Indonesia, and a PhD student in Media and Communication Studies at Universiti Malaya.

My work explores film and media studies, new media trends, popular culture, and environmental communication. I'm interested in how media represent identity, faith, gender, and everyday life.
